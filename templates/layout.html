Behind the numbers About this archive & the scoring

An independent fan project covering seasons {{ archive.first }}–{{ archive.last }} of the U.S. Dancing with the Stars. This is a historical archive, not a live results service. Not affiliated with ABC, Disney, or the show.

Average judge score is the mean of each contestant’s recorded weekly average judge scores, excluding zero and missing weeks. Score rank orders these averages within a season; tied scores share a rank. It does not model audience votes or prove who deserved to win.

Contestants compete for different numbers of weeks, and judging panels, scoring formats, and difficulty vary. Weekly totals are shown as recorded and may use different scales. Cross-season comparisons are descriptive, not adjusted for these differences.
